A companion for the phone that counts not the screen time but the use to no purpose: the apps opened by reflex (social networks, games, short video), as opposed to the ones used as tools. Each day in words: the openings and time of the reflex apps, what came first after unlocking, the longest stretch, the evening, the quick checks; a week page; a widget. Two interventions, off by default and never blocking: a pause with a question before a reflex app, a word after a while in one. Everything is read on the phone from Android's own usage events; no network permission, nothing leaves the phone.
